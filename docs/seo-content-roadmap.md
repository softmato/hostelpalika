What to write next, in order, and the brief every piece follows. Programmatic pages (cities, types,
areas, near-college, roles) are already generated from data; this file is for the pages a person
has to write. Nothing here is published until a person with the knowledge writes it — `/blog`
stays `noindex` until then.

## Brief template

- **Reader and intent** — who searches this and what they want to decide.
- **Primary question** and the related questions to answer on the same page.
- **Facts it needs** — each with its source (listing data, a product screen, a named person).
- **The Nepal-specific part** — what someone outside Nepal could not write.
- **Structure** — answer first in two sentences, then detail; one table where numbers compare.
- **Internal links** — the city/area/near/role pages and real hostel pages it should point to.
- **Author / reviewer** — a named person with first-hand knowledge; their role.
- **Last reviewed** — a date on the page.
- **Claims to verify before publishing** — every number, every "only", every comparison.
- **Call to action** — browse hostels, or list your hostel; one per piece.

## Rules

No invented surveys, statistics, quotes or authors. No page per keyword. A number on a guide page
comes from the listings at the time of writing and says so.
