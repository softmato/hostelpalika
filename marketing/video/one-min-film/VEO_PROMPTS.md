# One-minute film — Google Flow / Veo clips

Generate each in Flow (Veo, 16:9, 8 s, highest quality, **no text, no logos, no people's faces
unless stated**). Download the best take and save it with the exact filename into
`marketing/video/one-min-film/assets/veo/`. Colour rule: neutral / warm only — no red, blue or
lime grading; our green comes from the app itself.

## 1. `hook_ledger.mp4` — the 2-second hook (most important)
> Extreme macro close-up of an old, worn hostel rent register lying open on a dark wooden desk at
> night. Handwritten ink columns of names and rupee amounts, coffee ring stains, sticky notes and a
> cheap calculator beside it. The pages flutter, then the paper edges catch fire: glowing orange
> embers crawl across the page and curl it. Thick white smoke rises and swirls towards the lens
> until it completely fills the frame in the last second. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, moody
> tungsten light, slow push-in, 24 fps, photoreal.

## 2. `smoke_wipe.mp4` — transition overlay
> Dense white smoke billowing across a pure black background from left to right, swirling and
> filling the whole frame by the middle of the clip, then thinning out to reveal black again.
> Locked-off static camera, studio lighting, high contrast, no other objects. Photoreal, 24 fps.

## 3. `laptop_office.mp4` — the web-portal beat
> Locked-off static camera (absolutely no camera movement). A modern open laptop on a clean light
> wooden desk in a bright, calm small office in Kathmandu, morning sunlight through a window,
> a potted plant and a cup of tea beside it. The laptop screen faces the camera squarely and shows
> a flat, uniform pure green (#00FF00) screen, evenly lit, no reflections. Soft dust motes drift in
> the sunbeam. 24 fps, photoreal.

## 4. `owner_relaxed.mp4` — closing emotion
> A Nepali hostel owner in his forties, casual shirt, sitting relaxed on a rooftop terrace at golden
> hour with the Kathmandu valley and hills behind him. He glances at his phone (screen not visible
> to camera), smiles, and leans back calmly sipping tea. Slow cinematic dolly-in, warm sunset light,
> shallow depth of field, 24 fps, photoreal.

## 5. `particles_loop.mp4` — background texture (optional)
> Abstract soft bokeh particles and faint light leaks drifting slowly on a near-black background,
> warm white and very subtle emerald green glints, elegant and minimal, seamless slow motion,
> locked-off camera, 24 fps.

When the files are in place, tell Claude "veo clips ready".
